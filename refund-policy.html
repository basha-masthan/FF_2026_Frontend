Refund and Cancellation Policy

At Winzone Arena (FutureBound Tech), we strive to ensure a fair and transparent gaming environment. Please read our refund and cancellation policy carefully.

1. Tournament Cancellation by User

If you have registered for a tournament and wish to cancel, you may do so up to 15 minutes before the scheduled start time. In such cases, the full entry fee will be refunded to your Winzone Arena wallet instantly.

Cancellations made within 15 minutes of the start time or after the tournament has started are not eligible for a refund.

2. Tournament Cancellation by Admin

If a tournament is cancelled by the Winzone Arena admins due to technical issues, insufficient participation, or server maintenance, 100% of the entry fee will be refunded to all registered participants' wallets immediately.

3. Payment Failures

If money is deducted from your bank account but not reflected in your Winzone Arena wallet (Deposit failure), the amount is usually auto-refunded by the payment gateway within 5-7 business days. If not, please contact our support team with the transaction ID.

4. Disqualification

If a user is disqualified from a tournament due to cheating, hacking, or violation of fair play rules, NO refund of the entry fee will be provided.
